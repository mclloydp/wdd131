Mutare, Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in Southern Africa, home to Victoria Falls, one of the largest waterfalls in the world. Mutare, known as Umtali until 1982, is the capital and largest city in the province of Manicaland. It is the third-largest city in Zimbabwe. Zimbabwe has a growing community of developers and IT professionals working to expand the local tech industry.
